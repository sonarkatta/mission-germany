## Learning modules (read-and-understand)
- 10 topics x 2 modules x 3 paragraphs (~300-380 words each): concepts in plain English, written for an absolute beginner.
- Every module ends with a one-line "key" takeaway.
- 10 topic quizzes (6 questions each) with per-question explanations; accuracy feeds the adaptive coach.

## Practice surfaces
- Topic drills: 10/20/30 questions per topic, instant feedback + explanation.
- Full mocks: 3 fixed 180-question mocks (2 sessions x 2h15m, real exam timing) + unlimited random mocks.
- Quick Revision: 60 rapid flip-cards (6/topic: formulas + high-yield facts) + 30-minute cram auto-built from weakest topics.
